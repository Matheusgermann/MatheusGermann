# 👨🏻‍💻📈 Matheus Germann
**`Data Science & AI Engineering`** · Data Science student and intern at General Motors

## 🧠 About me

I'm a Data Science student and an intern at General Motors, where I build AI agents in production used by more than 20 people in Brazil and Mexico. Hands-on work in industrial automation and maintenance gave me a systemic view of complex problems, the same view I apply today to agents, LLMs and data pipelines built with Python, SQL and PySpark on Databricks.

I build solutions end to end: data pipelines, text-to-SQL agents evaluated (evals) against official KPIs, full-stack apps with React, TypeScript and FastAPI, and computer vision models with PyTorch and YOLO. I work every day with AI-assisted development: Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and MCP.

</br>

## 🏭 Experience highlights (General Motors)

- 🤖 **AssetGenie**: production AI agent (Databricks Genie + Glean) that turns natural-language questions into SQL over IBM MAXIMO maintenance data, with evals against official KPIs of 23 plants. **700+ questions** in the first two months at Gravataí, **20+ users** across Brazil and Mexico, global rollout approved. Estimated business case of **US$ 513k**. 🥈 **2nd place, Innovation Walk Q2 (GM)**.
- 📊 **AssetApp**: full-stack app on Databricks Apps (React, TypeScript, FastAPI, ECharts) unifying **10+ dashboards**, with Genie integration and a second LLM layer (Claude via API).
- 🔗 **SAPGenie**: agent and analytics layer over SAP ECC across **5 plants**, with a **98% match** between MAXIMO and SAP items.
- 📝 **Failure log**: Glean agents and Python routines integrated with Power Automate, Slack and SharePoint: **65+ real incidents**, reports to **114 recipients**.
